    About ZenFocus Pro

    ZenFocus Pro is an Android-exclusive, edge-to-edge cognitive flow workspace designed strictly for mobile touch interfaces.

    How to Use

    1. Set Your Target: Open the right menu, create an objective, and tap Anchor to bind it to the timer.
    2. Engage Sprint: Select 25m Deep Work and tap Start Sprint.
    3. Enable Flow Shield: Tap "Enable Audio Shield" to activate local procedural 40Hz acoustic masking.
    4. Track Velocity: View accumulated focus time and completed sprint cycles directly on your mobile dashboard.
